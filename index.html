"We are not defined by a single product. We are defined by a question — how much can technology truly understand about a person?"

The name Affyne is built from two foundational ideas: Affinity — human connection, trust, and empathy — and Intelligence — the ability to perceive, understand, and recognise patterns in context. Labs reflects our methodology: grounded exploration, clinical validation, and responsible deployment.

Our vision for Human Understanding Intelligence

Our mission

"To make technology understand people more deeply, and use that understanding to support human connection more meaningfully."

Technology has become increasingly capable of processing information — but people are more than information. Context, emotion, relationships, behaviour, and history all shape what someone communicates. Affyne Labs exists to account for these dimensions, responsibly.

Our vision

Human Understanding Intelligence.

We are building towards technology capable of interpreting human communication, context, behaviour, and evolving states — in ways that enable more meaningful human experiences and support. Mental health is our first domain of application. It is not the limit of what we intend to explore.

WHERE WE BEGIN

Understanding what lies behind a conversation.

Our first product direction is designed to understand a person's communication across conversation, voice, emotion, intent, context, personal baseline, and longitudinal patterns — and to use that understanding to provide personalised support, appropriate interventions, and human or professional escalation when required.

Mental health is one of the clearest areas where there is a gap between what a person experiences and what they are able or willing to say out loud. We are exploring how technology can understand what lies behind a conversation — not to replace human care, but to make support more contextual, timely, and personal.

The Understanding Pipeline

How technology learns to understand a person.

PHASE 01 // SIGNAL PERCEPTION
01 / SIGNAL PERCEPTION

Perceiving vocal cadence and micro-hesitation

Authentic dialogue carries acoustic signals that vocabulary alone obscures. Our speech acoustics research analyzes fundamental frequency (f₀), pitch trajectory, micro-pauses, and vocal timbre in real time to perceive genuine emotional state beneath semantic choices.

16 kHz Downsample Latency < 40ms f₀ Prosody Tracking
PHASE 02 // LONGITUDINAL NORMS
02 / LONGITUDINAL NORMS

Tracking individual baselines over time

People differ profoundly in how they communicate under stress. Rather than measuring individuals against generic population averages, our engine maps each person's unique multi-week baseline trajectory to detect statistically significant personal deviations.

PHASE 03 // CONTEXTUAL INGESTION
03 / CONTEXTUAL INGESTION

Decoupling overt words from unspoken intent

Communication is shaped by relational history, situational pressure, and what someone cannot bring themselves to say out loud. We build contextual models that account for paralinguistic tension even when spoken words seem calm.

How Human Understanding Intelligence works

End-to-end signal ingestion, normative baseline comparison, and real-time clinical escalation protocol.

STAGE 01

Multimodal Signal Ingestion

Listens beyond literal vocabulary. Analyzes speech intonation, hesitation cadence, vocal timbre, and conversation syntax during everyday check-ins.

STAGE 02

Longitudinal Context Engine

Builds an authentic personal baseline over weeks. Detects statistically meaningful deviations from an individual's emotional norm rather than generic population averages.

STAGE 03

Clinical Guardrails & Escalation

Maintains strict real-time safety thresholds (< 120ms triage latency). Automatically surfaces rich longitudinal context to licensed human care teams when escalation is required.

What we believe

Three principles that guide everything.

01

People are more than the words they say.

Communication carries layers that spoken or written words alone cannot convey — tone, context, pattern, history, and what is left unsaid. Technology that listens only to words misses what matters most.

02

Understanding should come before intervention.

Acting without understanding risks harm. We believe in building deep contextual awareness first — so that any support offered is appropriate, timely, and genuinely helpful to the individual.

03

Technology should augment human connection, not replace it.

The goal is not to substitute human care with software. It is to make human care more informed, more accessible, and more responsive — by giving it better context at the right moment.
