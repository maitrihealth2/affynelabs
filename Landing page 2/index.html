WHO WE ARE

Affinity meets intelligence.

Learn About Us

affynelabs is a HealthTech innovation company exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence, human understanding, and well-being.

We explore, research, and build technologies that help us understand people more deeply and enable better forms of support.

Our name brings together two ideas: affinity, representing human connection, trust, and empathy, and intelligence, representing the ability to understand patterns and context.
