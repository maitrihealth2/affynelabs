WHO WE ARE

Built on affinity. Guided by intelligence.

Affyne Labs is built from two ideas: Affinity — human connection, trust, attachment and empathy — and Intelligence — the ability to perceive, understand, and recognize patterns in context.

Labs reflects how we intend to build: through exploration, experimentation, research, and iteration.

Our Story
ABOUT AFFYNE LABS

A HealthTech company exploring human understanding.

Affyne Labs is a HealthTech innovation company exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence, human understanding, and wellbeing.

Our first application is mental health and emotional wellbeing — that is where we start, not where we stop.

OUR MISSION

Technology that understands people more deeply.

To make technology understand people more deeply, and use that understanding to support human connection more meaningfully.

Technology has become increasingly capable of processing information, but people are more than information — context, emotion, relationships, behaviour, and history all shape what someone communicates.

Affyne Labs exists to account for these dimensions, responsibly.

OUR VISION

Human Understanding Intelligence.

To build Human Understanding Intelligence — technology capable of interpreting human communication, context, behaviour, and evolving states, in ways that enable more meaningful human experiences and support.

Mental health is our first domain of application. It is not the limit of what we intend to explore.
