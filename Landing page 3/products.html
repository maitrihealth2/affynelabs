OUR PRODUCTS

Intelligent tools, guided by human understanding.

Affyne Labs is building privacy-first technologies that explore the intersection of artificial intelligence, human context, and wellbeing.

We are not defining the company by a single off-the-shelf product. We experiment, research, and build thoughtfully through iteration, exploring multiple technologies as our understanding of human needs deepens.

INITIAL FOCUS

Mental health & emotional wellbeing.

Mental health and emotional wellbeing is our first domain of application. That is where we start, not where we stop.

We are creating assistive intelligence capable of interpreting context, nuance, and evolving states—building tools designed to support everyday care, safe personal reflection, and meaningful human connection without compromising privacy.
